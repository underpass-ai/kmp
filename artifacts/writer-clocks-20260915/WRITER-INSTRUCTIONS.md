# Fresh writer protocol

Read [SOURCE.md](SOURCE.md) once, then work in a new isolated embedded KMP
store. Use the binary passed to the local driver; do not read a previous writer
store, copy a prior attempt, invoke a paid model, or use credentials.

Write source-backed memories in your own words. The protocol deliberately does
not prescribe a packet, ids, kinds, summaries, relation direction, or call
count. Preserve the distinction between R8, H8, the quantity-only correction,
and G2's unresolved disagreement when the source justifies it. Do not invent a
relation occurrence, validity, or an observation time that F17/G2 do not give.

When a rich relation returns `needs_review`, review the served stored/proposed
neighborhood before choosing whether and how to resume. Its clocks must be
readable. Use returned refs and continuations exactly; do not reconstruct them.
Inspect the accepted receipt and choose temporal reads appropriate to the
representation you actually wrote.

Record every request, response, validation error and final receipt through the
driver. A completed deterministic check establishes the supplied
representation's behavior. It does not establish source completeness or writer
understanding; report any mismatch or omission plainly.

# Fresh final-main writer protocol

Read `FRESH-SOURCE.md` once, then work through the supplied driver and its new
isolated embedded KMP store. Do not read previous writer reports, traces,
tests, issue discussions, or seed payloads. Do not copy another attempt or use
credentials. The driver performs no model call.

Write source-backed memories in your own words. Choose the packet, ids, kinds,
summaries, relation directions and call count from the source and KMP guidance.
Keep qualifications, negations, uncertainty and distinct clocks. Do not invent
relationship occurrence, validity, identity, causation or source facts.

The isolated store already contains older archive policy and measurement facts.
When a rich relation returns `needs_review`, inspect the literal stored and
proposed neighborhood, its clocks, selection reasons and omissions before
deciding whether to resume or correct the proposal. Follow opaque returned
continuations exactly. Expand context only through an action actually returned
by KMP. Inspect the accepted receipt and perform temporal reads appropriate to
the representation you wrote. Exercise an exact retry of an accepted logical
write without changing its idempotency key.

Record every request, response, rejection and continuation through the driver.
Report omissions and limitations plainly. Delivered context and successful
persistence do not prove comprehension, source completeness or causal
improvement.

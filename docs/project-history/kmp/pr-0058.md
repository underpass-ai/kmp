## Author's change description

Fixes #57. Found by the repository owner replaying their own memory: `24/24`, stopped, two thirds of the graph still dark.

## Counted, not guessed

```
nodes reached by wake: 68
with a temporal coordinate: 24     <- exactly the 24/24 on the playbar
without one: 44
relations into the dark ones: {has_dimension: 12, has_evidence: 31}
```

The 44 are **dimensions and evidence**. Not missing, not late — they carry no time of their own, so no timeline query can return them, and a replay built from a timeline could never light them.

## Why they belong in the replay

A dimension is the scope an entry was written into. Evidence exists at the moment the entry it supports does. Their time *is* the entry's time.

Leaving them dark says something false about the memory: that two thirds of it has no place in the story being replayed. And the screen said it louder than any reasoning — a replay that ends with most of the graph unlit looks like it stopped early, whatever the counter claims.

## The fix

A step reveals the entry **and whatever hangs off it** — `has_dimension`, `has_evidence`, `contains_entry`, `supports`, `records`. No timestamps are invented: attached nodes appear when the entry they belong to does, and the counter still counts entries, because entries are what the timeline holds.

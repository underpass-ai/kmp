Closes #82

## What

- retain short recall identifiers such as `PR`, `83`, `C1`, `M1`, `P0`, and single numeric tokens
- include evidence IDs and `supports` refs in the searchable evidence surface
- add regressions for tokenization, separated identifiers, and identity/support-only matches

## Why

The recall mapper previously discarded every token shorter than three characters and searched only evidence text, source, and metadata. Queries naming compact identifiers could therefore return `UNKNOWN` even when the exact stored evidence was present.

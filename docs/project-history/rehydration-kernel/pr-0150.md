## What

- **`MemoryCoordinateSpec.rank`**: not sequence's synonym — a distinct temporal axis in the kernel (`TemporalAxis::Rank`): sequence says *when among peers*, rank says *how high*. The kernel's one-based validation is inherited; a contract test proves `rank: Some(0)` is refused, non-transient.
- **`MemoryDimensionSpec.metadata`**: restores the symmetry entries and evidence already had; the index projection stamps its algorithm version on the dimension it declares.

## Why now

The embedding product's precedent candidate index publisher is migrating off deep `MemoryIngestCommand` imports onto `MemoryRecordApi` (ADR-0023's compiler-checked path). Its projection ranks candidate entries within the precedent dimension and stamps dimension metadata; without these two fields the migration would silently store less than it stores today.

## Author's change description

## What

Two commits that close what the last deep recall consumer needs:

1. **The kernel observes its own contract recalls.** `wake` and `ask` in the contract implementation now observe their result's quality into the kernel's telemetry, under the rpc names the telemetry has always carried (`kernel_wake`/`kernel_ask`). Quality observation had been the consumer's discipline — the embedding product's search adapter was the one consumer that remembered, deep-calling `quality_observer()` it should never have seen; a consumer that forgot would have silently starved the kernel's quality account. Proven end to end: contract wake → kernel dropped (guard flushes) → `RedbQualityTelemetryReader` holds the observation.

2. **`MemoryRelationshipView` gains `why` and `evidence`**, from the relation's explanation. A consumer surfacing a conflict quotes the recorded rationale instead of inventing one — exactly what the embedding product's conflict reporting does.

## Why together

Both are the recall-side remainder discovered by sizing that consumer's migration line by line; after this, its search adapter, bundle mapper and telemetry projector compile against the contract alone, and the only deep call left anywhere is `EmbeddedKernel::open` composition.

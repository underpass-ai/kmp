Views survive a full SQLite backup because their heads, revisions, and receipts
live in additive SQLite tables. They do **not** survive the canonical event-only
export/import bundle; that bundle restores canonical sources, after which views
must be authored again. This boundary is also specified in
`incremental-consolidation.md` and the generated consolidation guide.

The existing raw artifacts are retained because they prove both the successful
controls and their counterexamples. Re-running them without fresh independent
readers would not add acceptance evidence. In particular, this evaluation does
not prove a general quality gain or a lower total cost; it only reports the
observed control results and costs.
